What We Teach

Every member gets real coaching, not just a key to the door. Tell us your goal and Coach Clark will build the plan to get you there.

01

One-on-One Boxing Training

Personal coaching, one on one. We start where you are, fix what needs fixing and build your skills round by round.

  • Stance, punches and defense from the ground up
  • Pad and mitt work with your coach
  • Custom daily workouts
02

Competition Training

Want to step in the ring for real? We train fighters to compete, with hard spar days, fight conditioning and a coach who competes himself.

  • Regular spar days
  • Strength and conditioning mentorship
  • Tournament preparation
03

Self-Defense

Learn to protect yourself and carry yourself with confidence. Boxing teaches you to stay calm, keep your guard up and react under pressure.

  • Defense, blocking and slipping punches
  • Awareness and distance
  • Confidence that carries outside the gym
04

Footwork & Balance

Good boxing starts at your feet. Ladder drills, movement and balance work make you quicker, steadier and harder to hit.

  • Agility ladder and movement drills
  • Balance and core control
  • Getting in and out of range
05

Weight Loss & Conditioning

Boxing is one of the hardest workouts there is. Burn fat, build stamina and get stronger with workouts that keep you coming back.

  • High-energy conditioning rounds
  • Custom daily workouts
  • Box-Fit shakes and teas to fuel recovery
